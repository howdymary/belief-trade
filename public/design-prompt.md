# Belief Trade — a premium website design prompt

Act as a senior brand strategist, digital art director, motion designer, and front-end engineer. Develop a distinctive identity and build three complete, shareable websites for **Belief Trade**. The tagline is **Trade Belief.**

Create original compositions with the craft of an independent design studio: exceptional typography, deliberate art direction, carefully paced storytelling, satisfying interactions, and an unmistakable point of view. Deliver working websites rather than mood boards or descriptions.

## The story

**The future starts as someone’s belief.**

Before a technology becomes obvious, before an industry becomes consensus, someone sees the world differently. Belief Trade is a product concept for making that point of view tangible: select supported assets, define their proportions, name the thesis, and package the portfolio as a token other people can discover and potentially participate in.

The emotional promise: **Your portfolio can say something about the future.**

The practical promise: **An idea. A visible composition. One instrument.**

A creator expresses a thesis; a participant can understand its holdings and rules before deciding whether to participate. This is a concept for portfolio tokens, not a binary prediction market. Do not add odds, guaranteed outcomes, automatic profits, or a made-up dividend model.

Develop the narrative beyond generic democratization language. Make the connection between conviction, identity, curation, and participation understandable in one minute. “Anyone can express a belief” is the ambition; actual asset availability and eligibility would depend on the eventual product design. These sites are prototypes, with illustrative portfolios and no live trading, issuance, deposits, wallet connection, or signup collection.

## Three genuinely different creative directions

### 01 — Editorial / horizontal scrollytelling

Study https://www.grids.obys.agency for grid discipline, typography, pacing, and scale. Borrow principles, not its assets or exact layout.

Use an oversized typographic opening, a strong modular grid, electric chartreuse against ink and paper, and an editorial rhythm. On desktop, the story moves horizontally through four or five purposeful chapters. Provide visible chapter controls, keyboard navigation, touch support, and a vertical mobile experience. Keep the opening recognizable and readable before any animation runs.

The story moves from “Trade Belief” to why individual conviction matters, then to how a portfolio expresses it, then to the visitor’s own point of view. Let scale, composition, and transitions do the work. Avoid generic rows of identical feature cards.

### 02 — Studio / clean and premium

Reinterpret the same grid and typography discipline as a calm vertical site. Use bright white, deliberate blue, elegant serif moments, a restrained sans serif, generous space, fine rules, and subtle motion.

Make the idea easy to understand. Show the proposed composition of one portfolio as an editorial product demonstration. Use only the sections needed to tell the story, a considered portfolio discovery moment, and a memorable closing invitation. Premium means precise spacing, excellent proportions, and restraint—not decorative gradients, excessive glass, or an empty hero.

### 03 — World / nostalgic and adorable

Create an original cozy online world inspired by the emotional qualities of early-2000s Neopets and MapleStory: discovery, belonging, charming characters, tactile interfaces, and a sense of a small world worth returning to.

Use original pixel-art or illustrated scenery, warm game-like surfaces, expressive display type, and highly readable body text. Make the site feel like entering a place. Let the mascot guide a journey from a small idea to a portfolio and a community of fellow believers. Keep the financial concept legible beneath the charm. Do not copy existing game characters, logos, music, or assets.

## Mascot

Create an original adorable mascot, **Bibi**: a small cream-colored rabbit-like spirit with floppy ears, peach cheeks, a tiny gold star on its forehead, and a green neckerchief. Bibi is curious, optimistic, and a little earnest. Its personality is “Small bun. Big beliefs.”

Produce art appropriate to each direction while preserving recognizable character traits: a tactile collectible mascot for the premium pages and a pixel-art adventurer for the nostalgic world. Generate clean assets without embedded UI or headings; render website text as accessible HTML. Use the mascot in a few deliberate places, not everywhere.

## Content and interactions

Use the fixed project name and tagline. Develop concise original copy. Every chapter should add an idea rather than repeat the headline.

Illustrative portfolio themes can include AI infrastructure, open finance, and energy progress. Show transparent example allocations that total 100%, and label them as illustrative. Do not invent performance charts, customers, user counts, endorsements, revenue, live prices, audits, or market availability.

Give every visible control a useful outcome. “Explore a belief” can reveal its thesis and example holdings. “Our story” can open a concise manifesto. Do not create fake transaction success messages, pretend subscriptions, or nonfunctional social links. Avoid asking for emails without a working, explicitly requested collection flow.

Include a small comparison gallery and separate routes so each direction is independently shareable. Allow visitors to move between designs without overwhelming the brand experience. Each direction must have different composition, pacing, typography, imagery, and interaction—not just a different palette.

## Craft requirements

- Design the first viewport and full scrolling sequence together.
- Choose fonts intentionally and provide robust fallbacks.
- Use responsive type and spacing; keep main body copy readable on small screens.
- Provide real focus states, semantic headings, descriptive controls, image alternatives, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep content available without animation; avoid forced intros, loading theatrics, scroll traps, and tiny labels carrying essential meaning.
- Optimize the generated imagery and preserve transparent backgrounds.
- Prefer a small coherent implementation to unnecessary dependencies.
- Use fine-grained, purposeful motion. Avoid indiscriminate bouncing, parallax, and cursor effects.
- Make mobile a designed experience, not a compressed desktop page.
- Keep investment risk and prototype status understandable without filling the story with legal boilerplate.

## Delivery

Build the three routes and comparison gallery. Include this reusable brief, a short narrative rationale, and image-generation prompts in the project. Run the production build and validate routes, assets, accessibility basics, and interaction logic. If browser testing is available and authorized, inspect desktop and mobile and correct actual problems before publication.

Publish the finished concepts on public GitHub Pages in a dedicated repository using the authenticated owner. Publish only this website project; exclude unrelated workspace documents, secrets, and local configuration. Verify the deployment before claiming it is live. Return one comparison link and a direct link for each design. Clearly identify any unresolved blocker.

Make decisions and continue independently. The result should feel authored, emotionally engaging, and specific to Belief Trade.
